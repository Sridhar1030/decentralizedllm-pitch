Decentralised inference · design in progress

One model, split across the devices you already own.

Open source models are really good now, but running one yourself is still expensive. You either buy a serious GPU, or you rent a cluster and send your prompts off to someone else's servers.

See how it works

What it is

A language model is a stack of layers. DecentralizedLLM would cut that stack into pieces and give one piece to each device a small team already has. A phone, a laptop, an old desktop with a decent GPU.

Your prompt goes to the first device, which runs its layers and passes the half finished result to the next one over wifi or a VPN. The last device turns it into a word and sends it back. Every device sees a few layers and nothing else, so no single one of them ever holds the full model.

Why that helps

  • The model no longer has to fit on one machine

    This is the whole point. A 70B model in int4 is about 35 GB, which fits on nothing normal. Split across five laptops it is roughly 7 GB each, which is ordinary.

  • You use hardware you already own

    No new GPU, no cluster to rent. The machines are already sitting there and already switched on.

  • The devices do not have to match

    A piece is just a range of layers, so the machine with the GPU takes more of them and the phone takes fewer. You pool whatever the group happens to own.

  • Your prompts stay on your own network

    Everything travels between your devices over your wifi or your VPN. Nothing is sent to a service you do not control.
